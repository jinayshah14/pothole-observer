Privacy policy

Pothole Observer (POT) Android app and server · effective 9 October 2026 · app notice version 2026-10-g

In short. While a trip is recording, POT uses your phone's motion sensors and location to find suspected potholes and speed breakers. Rides are kept on your phone and, if sharing is on, on the POT server, where they build a shared pothole map. Other riders see only pothole points, never names or routes. Signing in with Google is optional. POT never uses your camera, microphone or contacts. Nothing is sold and there are no ads.

Who runs POT

Pothole Observer is a test project run from github.com/jinayshah14/pothole-observer. It is a prototype: it does not measure how deep a pothole is, and it cannot feel holes your wheels never cross.

What the app records, and when

Only while a trip is recording (a notification shows the whole time):

  • the motion sensors: acceleration, rotation and related sensors;
  • your location and speed;
  • the phone model and Android version;
  • a random install id made by the app (not your phone's serial number, IMEI or advertising id);
  • the bumps you review and the choices you make in the app.

What is never collected

Camera, microphone, contacts and your phone number. The app has no permission to use the camera or microphone. POT never sees your Google password.

Signing in with Google (optional)

Recording, sharing and the map all work without an account. If you choose to sign in, Google gives POT your name, your email address, a Google account number and a link to your profile photo.

  • POT keeps your name, email address and Google account number in its account database, separately from rides. It does not keep your photo or its link.
  • They are used only to sign you in, to keep your rides together when you change phones or reinstall, to show you your own rides, and to write to you about your account, a data breach or a request you made.
  • No newsletters, no ads, never sold. Other riders never see your name or email.
  • Signing in links your phone to your account. The rides it has sent, and the ones it sends later, are added to your account. Only you, and the people who run the POT server, can see your account's rides.

What is shared, and with whom

  • Sent to the POT server when sharing is on, about once a minute while you ride: bump points (place, type and how sure the phone was), the route with its first and last 200 m cut off (those never leave the phone), the vehicle type, the phone model, the random install id and your choices.
  • Other riders see only the suspected spots on the shared map, as points: no names, no routes.
  • The people who run the POT server can see shared routes and accounts, to check the map and fix problems.
  • Service providers that store or carry the data for POT:
    • Render (server, Singapore);
    • Neon (database);
    • Hugging Face (private backup of full recordings);
    • Google (sign-in, only if you use it);
    • GitHub (this website).
    The map in the app and on this site loads map images from OpenFreeMap, which sees your internet address and the map area you look at.

Features that are off until you turn them on

  • Travel reminders: they use Android's "Physical activity" signal while POT is closed, and may sample motion for up to 2.5 minutes to check for road travel. No location is used until a trip starts.
  • Instant mode: it uses your location in the background (Android's "Allow all the time"), so it can start recording by itself when you leave a place where your rides often start or end, or when the phone moves at vehicle speed. A start that turns out not to be a vehicle stays on the phone, is never sent, and is cleared after 30 days.
  • Improve detection with my rides: it lets your reviewed rides be used to improve the detector.
  • Test-version extras: live position about once a minute while a trip records, and a full recording of each ride (all motion sensors and the whole route, including its start and end). These are for testers, and you can see and turn them off in the app under You › Your data.

How long data is kept

  • Rides are kept on your phone and, once shared, on the POT server, where they keep building the map. POT does not delete shared rides.
  • Account details (name, email, Google account number) are kept until you close your account.
  • Closing your account (in the app: You › Account) removes your name, email, Google account number and the link between your account and your phones, and signs you out on every phone. Your rides are kept on the server under the phone's random install id, with no name or email, and pothole points already on the map stay. The phone then starts a fresh random id.

Your choices and requests

  • In the app, under You › Your data, you can stop sending rides, turn off live position and full recordings, turn reminders and model improvement on or off, and see a dated history of every choice.
  • You can sign out, or close your account, at any time in You › Account.
  • To ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or anything else about your data, contact us (below). We reply within 30 days.

Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Each phone proves itself to the server with its own secret. Sign-in sessions are stored only as one-way hashes, and account details are kept apart from rides and are not part of the ride backup.

Age

Recording rides and signing in are only for people aged 18 or older. Anyone can look at the pothole map.

Changes to this policy

When what POT collects or shares changes, this page and the in-app notice are updated, and the app shows the change before you continue.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data (a copy, a correction, closing your account, or a complaint): email jinayshah1404@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.
