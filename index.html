The road, felt by a phone.

An Android app spots potholes and speed breakers from motion sensors alone. Each ride is saved to the cloud as you go, and every ride feeds one shared map.

It reminds you to start

The phone notices it is in a vehicle and checks the road for a moment. If you are not recording, it rings for 2 seconds, even on silent. Your car kit, scooter or helmet connecting over Bluetooth can set it off too.

Saved to the cloud as you ride

A ride is sent in pieces about every minute while you ride, so a dead phone or a crash loses nothing. Failed uploads retry by themselves, and the Rides tab shows "Saved to cloud".

The route follows the road

GPS jumps are dropped, stops no longer leave a star of dots, gaps stay open instead of becoming straight lines, and corners are kept. On the owner's replayed rides, the wander at stops fell from 267 m to 20 m over 69 stops.

From raw numbers to a jolt

up-and-down acceleration trigger line

The phone's tilt is removed using gravity, leaving only the up-and-down motion. A jolt above the trigger line becomes a candidate.
